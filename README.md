# 指映 FretReflect

> 指板映像，一目了然。

一个面向吉他初学者的个人乐理速查工具，将音阶、指板音位、和弦构成与指法集中在一页，随时查看。

---

## 功能一览

### 🎵 基础音阶
展示一个完整八度内的 12 个半音。自然音为方形卡片，半音为圆形卡片，标注升降双名和唱名。

### 🎸 指板音位图
标准调弦（EADGBE）下，0 品到 12 品所有音位一目了然。左侧空弦音以彩色方框高亮。

### 🎼 常见和弦
内置 10 个初学者常用和弦（C、D、Dm、E、Em、F、G、A、Am、B7），展示构成音和按法指位图。

### 🔗 和弦投射
点击「投射」按钮，可将当前和弦的按法投射到指板音位图上，同时高亮音阶中的构成音，直观理解和弦与指板、音阶的关系。

---

## 如何使用

1. 打开网页即可浏览全部内容
2. 在「常见和弦」板块选择和弦，查看构成音和指法
3. 点击「投射」按钮，观察该和弦在指板上的按法位置和音阶中的构成音
4. 再次点击「取消」恢复默认视图

# FretReflect

> Fretboard reflection, clear at a glance.

A personal music theory reference tool for guitar beginners. Scales, fretboard note positions, chord composition, and fingerings — all on one page.

---

## Features

### 🎵 Basic Scale
Displays all 12 notes in an octave. Natural notes shown as square cards, half-steps as circular cards, with both sharp and flat names plus solfège.

### 🎸 Fretboard Note Map
Full note layout from fret 0 to 12 under standard tuning (EADGBE). Open string notes highlighted with colored square markers on the left.

### 🎼 Common Chords
10 beginner-friendly chords built in (C, D, Dm, E, Em, F, G, A, Am, B7). Shows chord composition notes and fingering diagrams.

### 🔗 Chord Projection
Tap the "Project" button to overlay the selected chord's fingering onto the fretboard, while highlighting its constituent notes on the scale — making the relationship between chords, fretboard, and scales instantly visible.

---

## How to Use

1. Open the page to browse all content
2. Select a chord in the "Common Chords" section to view its notes and fingering
3. Click "Project" to see the chord's fingering on the fretboard and its notes highlighted on the scale
4. Click "Cancel" to restore the default view
